## 1.2. Project Overview & Objectives
This project is an end-to-end market research and competitive intelligence analysis of two distinct global technology markets: the **Smartphone Market** and the **Cloud Infrastructure Services Market**. 

The objective of this project was to simulate the type of analytical work carried out by technology market research teams (such as Gartner or IDC). Rather than simply creating a sales dashboard or analysing company financial statements, this project approaches the data from a market research perspective. It focuses on defining entire markets, sizing the addressable opportunity, evaluating vendor performance, calculating market shares, measuring competitive concentration, and translating historical data into strategic forecasts.

## 1.4. Data Sources & Methodology

### Market Definitions
- **Smartphone Market:** Global shipments of smartphones (mobile devices with an advanced operating system supporting third-party applications). Primary metric: Unit Shipments. Secondary metric: Revenue (where comparable). Geographic scope: Global.
- **Cloud Infrastructure Market:** Global enterprise spending on Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS), Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS), and hosted private cloud. Excludes Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) and consulting. Primary metric: Vendor Revenue. Geographic scope: Global.

### Vendors Included
- **Smartphones (7 vendors):** Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi, OPPO, Vivo, Transsion (Tecno/Infinix/itel), Lenovo/Motorola.
- **Cloud Infrastructure (6 vendors):** AWS (Amazon Web Services), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Alibaba Cloud, Oracle Cloud, IBM Cloud.

### Time Period
- Historical Data: 2020–2024 (5 calendar years).
- Forecast Horizon: 2025–2027.

### Primary Data Sources
- **Smartphones:** IDC Worldwide Quarterly Mobile Phone Tracker (Primary), Canalys, Counterpoint Research (Cross-validation).
- **Cloud Infrastructure:** Synergy Research Group (Primary).

### Source Classification
Every figure in the project is classified in the Source Register as one of the following:
- **Reported:** Directly stated by a named source.
- **Calculated:** Derived from reported figures via explicit arithmetic (e.g., YoY growth, market share).
- **Estimated:** Based on stated assumptions (e.g., forecasts, TAM/SAM/SOM, missing vendor revenue).

## 1.5. Data Processing & Calculation Logic (Python)
All data cleaning, processing, and exploratory analysis were conducted in Python using `pandas` and `numpy`. 

Key calculation logic includes:
- **Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth:** Calculated by comparing the current year's metric to the previous year's metric for each vendor individually.
- **Market Share (%):** Calculated by dividing a vendor's metric (shipments or revenue) by the total market metric for that specific year.
- **CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate):** Used to measure the historical growth of the overall Cloud market from 2020 to 2024.
- **Data Visualization:** Generated using `matplotlib` and `seaborn` to identify historical market trends and share shifts.

## 1.6. SQL Database & Analytical Queries
A relational database was built using SQLite to demonstrate structured data querying. The database consists of `smartphone_data` and `cloud_data` tables. 

Analytical SQL queries were written using advanced functions:
- **Window Functions & Ranking:** Utilized `RANK() OVER (ORDER BY ...)` to rank vendors by market share in the most recent year.
- **Aggregations:** Used `AVG()` and `GROUP BY` to find the fastest-growing cloud vendors over the 5-year period.
- **CTEs and Self-Joins:** Used Common Table Expressions (CTEs) and self-joins to compare a vendor's market share in 2022 vs. 2024, isolating which companies gained or lost the most competitive ground.

## 1.7. Market Sizing (TAM, SAM, SOM)
Market sizing was performed to understand the addressable opportunity for Apple in the 2024 smartphone market. We did not use arbitrary percentages; instead, we built a bottom-up model with explicit assumptions and a sensitivity analysis.

- **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** 1,244 million units (Total global smartphone shipments).
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market):** 311 million units (Assuming the premium segment, which Apple targets, represents 25% of the total market).
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):** 232.4 million units (Apple's actual 2024 shipments).

**Result:** Apple captured **74.7%** of its Serviceable Addressable Market. A sensitivity analysis revealed that even if the premium segment is only 20% of the total market, Apple's capture rate rises to an astonishing **93.4%**.

## 1.8. Competitive Benchmarking & Concentration
To evaluate the competitive dynamics of both markets, we calculated the Four-Firm Concentration Ratio (CR4) and the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) for 2024.

- **Smartphone Market:** CR4 = 58.74%, HHI = 1085.75.
  - *Interpretation:* A highly competitive, fragmented market (HHI < 1500). No single vendor has an unbreakable monopoly, and smaller challengers (like Transsion) can successfully gain share.
- **Cloud Infrastructure Market:** CR4 = 72.67%, HHI = 1778.15.
  - *Interpretation:* A moderately concentrated oligopoly (HHI 1500–2500). The top 4 providers (AWS, Microsoft, Google, Alibaba) completely dominate the market, creating high barriers to entry.

## 1.9. Forecasting & Scenario Analysis
A 3-year forecast (2025–2027) was developed for the Cloud Infrastructure market. Because the smartphone market is mature and relatively flat (1-2% annual growth), forecasting was focused on the high-growth cloud market to provide actionable strategic intelligence.

Using the historical 2020–2024 Cloud market CAGR of **26.26%**, three scenarios were modeled:
- **Base Case:** Assumes sustained historical average growth.
- **Bull Case:** Assumes AI adoption accelerates growth by 5% above the historical average.
- **Bear Case:** Assumes economic slowdown and increased competitive pricing drop growth 5% below the historical average.

**Projections for 2027:**
- **Bear Case:** $589.1 Billion
- **Base Case:** $665.1 Billion
- **Bull Case:** $747.2 Billion

## 1.11. Key Findings & Strategic Insights

- **The Smartphone Market is a Bloodbath:** With a highly fragmented market (HHI: 1085), Apple managed to secure the #1 spot in 2024 (18.7% share), narrowly overtaking Samsung (18.0%). However, the real story is **Transsion** (Tecno/Infinix/itel), which gained a massive **+2.33%** market share between 2022 and 2024. Samsung lost the most ground (-3.69%).
- **The Cloud Market is an Oligopoly:** AWS (32.6%), Microsoft Azure (22.7%), and Google Cloud (13.0%) command over 68% of the market. The HHI of 1778 proves it is incredibly difficult for new players to enter. 
- **Growth Rates Favor the Challengers:** While AWS is the largest cloud provider, Oracle Cloud (35.4%) and Google Cloud (35.1%) had the highest average YoY growth rates from 2020 to 2024. AWS grew at an average of 24.4%.
- **The Cloud Market is Not Slowing Down:** Even in a pessimistic "Bear Case" scenario, the cloud market is projected to grow from $330.4 Billion in 2024 to over $589 Billion by 2027.
